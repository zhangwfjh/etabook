# Callouts

> Obsidian-style callout boxes, one per supported type.

## Positive / informational

> [!note] Note
> A general note with a pencil icon.

> [!info] Info
> Informational, blue accent.

> [!tip] Tip
> Helpful advice.

> [!success] Success
> Something completed.

> [!check] Check
> Verified.

## Questions and examples

> [!question] Question
> What does this do?

> [!example] Example
> A worked example.

> [!quote] Quote
> "Simplicity is the soul of efficiency." — Austin Freeman

## Warnings and errors

> [!warning] Warning
> Be careful here.

> [!caution] Caution
> Proceed with caution.

> [!danger] Danger
> This can destroy data.

> [!bug] Bug
> Known issue.

> [!failure] Failure
> Operation failed.

## Title defaults (no title given)

> [!todo]
> A to-do callout using the default title.

> [!abstract]
> Abstract / TL;DR using the default title.

> [!seealso]
> See-also callout using the default title.
