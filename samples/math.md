# Math

> Inline and display math rendered with KaTeX.

## Inline math

The quadratic formula is $x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}$ and the area of a circle is $\pi r^2$.

## Display math

$$
\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} e^{-x^2}\,dx = \sqrt{\pi}
$$

## Aligned equations

$$
\begin{aligned}
  f(x) &= (x + 1)^2 \\
       &= x^2 + 2x + 1
\end{aligned}
$$

## Matrix

$$
\begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix} x \\ y \end{pmatrix}
=
\begin{pmatrix} ax + by \\ cx + dy \end{pmatrix}
$$
